# S7 — Independent Review

Purpose: decide whether the exact current implementation is worth becoming a merge candidate.

Review:

- Goal/Spec coverage;
- actual Diff and impact;
- unrelated changes;
- security and performance;
- maintainability and architecture;
- test adequacy;
- known limitations.

Finding format:

```text
Severity:
File:Line:
Evidence:
Impact:
Recommendation:
```

No Blocker may remain.

A Reviewer may be the same product in a fresh context or the other product. Independence comes from clean evidence and rubric, not brand alone.
