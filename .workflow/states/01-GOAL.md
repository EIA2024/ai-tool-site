# S1 — Goal

Purpose: convert the user's request into a bounded, verifiable Goal.

Required outputs:

- desired outcome;
- success criteria;
- scope and non-goals;
- constraints and assumptions;
- initial risk;
- Goal version.

Forbidden:

- business-code changes;
- detailed implementation;
- self-approval.

Exit:

```text
APPROVE GOAL <run-id> vN
```
